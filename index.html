When to use this

Crisis

Production issue, 12-hour days, a family emergency, or a trip. The aim is to keep the habit going this week, so a few small sessions are enough.

Keep protein high and walk. Next week, go straight back to green.

Go in order, not by date

Missed Tuesday? Do Pull on Wednesday and keep going. If the week runs out, drop Day 4 (Upper) first.

8 min warm-up

5 min brisk walk at 6–7 km/h plus arm circles and hip openers. Move your 10 min run at 8 km/h to the end so it doesn't eat your lifting energy.

Add reps, then weight

Choose a weight you can lift for 8 reps with 2 left in the tank. Once you hit the top of the range on every set, add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (legs).

Log every set

Use Hevy, Strong or phone notes. If you can't see last week's numbers, you're guessing. Rest 90 s on most sets and 2–3 min on squat, bench and deadlift.

Supplements · fewer than you think

Four bottles, plus one pile to skip

Supplements cover the last 5%. Food, sleep and showing up cover the other 95%. Here's what's worth buying.

WheyYou have it

1 scoop after every workout. Add a 2nd scoop only on days you fall short of 150 g.

CreatineBuy · ~100 AED

Plain monohydrate, 3–5 g every day including rest days. No loading phase. The most proven supplement for strength. Drink more water with it.

Vitamin D3Test first

Indoor IT job plus a night shift means almost no sun. Most people in your situation are low. Take a blood test. Common maintenance is 1,000–2,000 IU with a meal. If you're low, the doctor sets the dose.

Omega-3Fish first

Eat fish twice a week (sardine, salmon, vanjaram, mackerel) and you can skip the capsule. If not, take 1 g of EPA+DHA daily. Check that number on the label, not "fish oil 1000 mg".

Don't buy

This week: one blood test (about 150–300 AED in a lab package)

Vitamin D (25-OH), B12, CBC, lipid profile, HbA1c, TSH. You get a baseline now and a retest in 6 months. Show the results to a doctor before taking high-dose anything.

Real life will happen

What if…

Each break you took started with one of these. Decide now what you'll do when it happens.

Work explodes this week

Switch to the busy or crisis plan. Protein, water and steps still count. A 20 min home session counts too.

Slept at 4 AM

Sleep in and skip the gym. Walk 30 min instead. With under 5 hours of sleep, don't lift heavy.

Lazy morning

The 10-minute deal: shoes on, walk the 150 m, do the warm-up and the first exercise. If you still feel dead, go home guilt-free. You'll almost always stay.

Fever or body ache

Rest fully. With only a runny nose, a light session is fine. With anything in the chest or a fever, stay home.

Missed a whole week

Come back at 80% of your weights on the same plan. No punishment cardio and no "restart Monday".

Office biryani or party

Eat one full plate and enjoy it. Skip seconds and dessert. Your next meal is normal. 80% clean is enough.

Sore everywhere (week 1)

Still go. Warm up, go light, do some cardio. Soreness fades by the 3rd session, and stopping makes it come back.

Scale stuck 2 weeks

Check your waist first, since you may be building muscle. If the waist is stuck too, cut 50 g rice a day or add 2,000 steps.

Motivation drops 10 → 4

It will, probably around week 5. Keep the session and shrink it to 30 min. Never miss twice in a row.

Football night

An intense hour counts as your engine session and burns about 600–700 kcal. Eat a full dinner that day. The next day is rest, and you continue the plan in order after that.

UAE heat, sweating a lot

Add 500 ml water per gym hour and a pinch of salt or an electrolyte sachet. Dark urine means drink more.

January: back to India

Plan this now. Use home dumbbells or a local gym with the busy-week plan. Eggs, curd, paneer, chicken and sundal travel well.
